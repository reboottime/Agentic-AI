Generative AI always wants
to try to solve the problem, and this leads to hallucination. We've all heard
about hallucination. This is when it tries to come up with an
answer that fits. It may look really convincing, but maybe it's not
factually accurate. Often, the cause of hallucination is that the
generative AI does not have the information that
it needs in order to accurately solve the problem
or answer or whatever it is. One thing that's really, really important when we're
building a custom GPT, particularly if we want it
to be factually accurate, but also if we just
want it to be better at solving problems or
more creative and inspirational is we
need to make sure it has enough information
to do a good job. We always want the information
to come before the problem-solving because
if we attempt to solve the problem without
getting the information first, what we're going to do
is either be at risk of hallucination or
we're going to be at risk of just producing a poor answer that isn't
exciting or isn't very good. There's a number of different
ways that we can do this. One, we can do things

custom GPT documents. But often we have to think about collecting other
types of information, and it's helpful to just quickly think through
some of those. What are some things

and describe the problem, but they may not
