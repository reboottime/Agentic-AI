# Helping Humans Solve Problems vs. Providing Answers

## Key Learning Points

### Core Philosophy: Augmented Intelligence
- **Goal**: Help human beings solve problems, not replace their reasoning
- **Approach**: Support human reasoning and decision-making
- **Outcome**: Help people think more, not less

### Why Focus on Problem-Solving Support
- **Prevent Over-Dependence**: Avoid users becoming dependent on AI for all thinking
- **Maintain Human Agency**: Keep users engaged in the reasoning process
- **Build Better Solutions**: Collaborative problem-solving leads to better outcomes
- **Avoid "Cheating" Culture**: Prevent users from bypassing critical thinking

### Implementation Strategies
- **Provide Information**: Give users relevant data and perspectives
- **Support Reasoning**: Help users work through problems step-by-step
- **Augment Intelligence**: Work together with humans, not replace them
- **Control Information Flow**: Manage what information is presented and how

### Key Techniques
- Present information in ways that require user engagement
- Structure responses to encourage thinking
- Provide context and perspectives rather than final answers
- Guide users through reasoning processes

### Benefits
- Users stay engaged and learn
- Better decision-making through collaboration
- Reduced risk of over-reliance on AI
- More thoughtful and considered outcomes

## Key Takeaway
The goal is to create custom GPTs that help people solve problems through augmented intelligence - working together with humans to provide information and support reasoning, rather than simply providing answers that bypass human thinking.
