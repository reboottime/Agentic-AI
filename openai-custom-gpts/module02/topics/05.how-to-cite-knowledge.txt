to give them answers. Now, there's a bunch of reasons
why we want to do that. We we want to focus
on helping them solve a problem as opposed

solve the problem, a big part of what
we're trying to do is to help them reason, help them arrive
at the solution, not replace their reasoning. A lot of what we saw
when we started with generative AI was that we ran into problems because
