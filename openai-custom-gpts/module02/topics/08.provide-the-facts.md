# Providing the Facts: Information Before Decision-Making

## Key Learning Points

### Core Principle: Information First
- **Prevent Hallucination**: Ensure GPT has enough information before attempting to solve problems
- **Better Quality**: Information gathering leads to more accurate and useful responses
- **Reduce Risk**: Avoid poor answers that aren't factually accurate

### Why Information Collection is Critical
- **Context Matters**: Answers vary dramatically based on context
- **User Preferences**: Different users have different needs and learning styles
- **Past Interactions**: Previous conversations may contain crucial context
- **Desired Outcomes**: Users may want different response formats or depths

### Types of Information to Collect
1. **Context About the Problem**: Full scope of where the problem occurs
2. **User Preferences**: Learning style, interaction model, desired format
3. **Past Interactions**: History outside current conversation
4. **Desired Outcome**: Short/concise vs. verbose, list vs. paragraph format

### Implementation Strategy
- **Explicit Instructions**: Tell GPT to collect information first
- **Don't Assume**: Avoid jumping to conclusions or making assumptions
- **Restate and Confirm**: Make information explicit and get user agreement
- **Information Before Action**: Only attempt to solve after gathering context

### Best Practices
- Always ensure sufficient information before problem-solving
- Make information collection explicit in GPT instructions
- Restate collected information for user confirmation
- Don't jump the gun or make assumptions

## Key Takeaway
Generative AI should always collect sufficient information before attempting to solve problems. This prevents hallucination, improves response quality, and ensures the GPT has the context needed to provide accurate and useful assistance.
