# Where to Get Additional Help: Human Support Integration

## Key Learning Points

### Human Support Integration
- **Escape Valve**: Always provide human contact options
- **Intelligent Routing**: Direct users to appropriate experts
- **Resource Connection**: Connect users to external resources
- **Support Ecosystem**: Don't rely solely on generative AI

### Implementation Strategy
- **End-of-Response Contacts**: Include relevant contacts after every answer
- **Context-Aware Routing**: Direct users based on their specific question
- **Multiple Support Channels**: Email, phone, tickets, FAQs, web pages
- **Expert Connection**: Route to domain experts when appropriate

### Types of Support Resources
1. **Human Experts**: Advisors, specialists, managers
2. **External Resources**: FAQs, web pages, documentation
3. **Support Systems**: Tickets, help desks, contact forms
4. **Educational Materials**: Books, guides, training resources

### Benefits
- **User Confidence**: Users know where to go for additional help
- **Reduced Frustration**: Clear path when AI can't fully solve the problem
- **Expert Access**: Connect users with human expertise
- **Comprehensive Support**: Multiple channels for different needs

### Design Considerations
- **Context-Appropriate**: Route based on question type and complexity
- **Multiple Options**: Provide various ways to get help
- **Clear Instructions**: Tell users exactly what to do
- **Escalation Path**: Clear progression from AI to human support

### Use Cases
- **Complex Questions**: When AI reaches its limits
- **User Preference**: When users prefer human interaction
- **Verification**: When users want to confirm AI responses
- **Specialized Knowledge**: When domain expertise is needed

## Key Takeaway
Always provide users with clear paths to human support and external resources. Custom GPTs should act as routers and connectors, helping users get to the right person or resource when they need additional help beyond what the AI can provide.
