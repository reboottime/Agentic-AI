# Enforcing Boundaries and Still Helping with the Alternative Approaches Pattern

## The Problem

There's a tension between:
- **Being helpful**: Wanting to help users solve their problems
- **Managing risk**: Not wanting to provide unreliable answers that could lead to financial or legal consequences

When questions have ambiguity or multiple interpretations, we need a way to:
- Avoid giving potentially wrong answers
- Still provide value to users
- Help them achieve their goals within safe boundaries

## The Solution

Use the **Alternative Approaches Pattern** to suggest clearly allowable alternatives when the original request is ambiguous.

### The Prompt

```md
Always start by analyzing the user's question and determine if the question can be clearly answered without multiple interpretations using the travel policy.

If not, suggest alternative approaches that the user could use to accomplish the same goal that would clearly be allowable in the travel policy.

Output in this format:
- The answer is unclear
- Instead of [user's request], you might try:
  1. [Clearly allowable option 1]
  2. [Clearly allowable option 2] 
  3. [Clearly allowable option 3]
```

### How It Works

1. **Analysis**: Determine if the question can be clearly answered
2. **Detection**: Identify when there's ambiguity or multiple interpretations
3. **Alternative Suggestions**: Propose clearly allowable alternatives
4. **Goal Achievement**: Help users reach their underlying goal safely

### Example

**User asks:** "Can I purchase Southwest Business Select?"

**GPT responds:**
"The answer is unclear.

Instead of purchasing Southwest Business Select, you might try:

1. Booking the lowest available economy class airfare that meets your travel needs
2. Utilizing allowable upgrade options like priority boarding or early check-in if they are necessary for your travel and conform to the guidelines
3. Discussing with your supervisor or the finance department whether exceptions can be made for specific circumstances that require higher class fares and getting such approvals in writing if they are granted"

### Benefits

- **Risk Management**: Avoids providing potentially wrong answers
- **User Helpfulness**: Still helps users achieve their goals
- **Clear Boundaries**: Only suggests clearly allowable options
- **Problem Solving**: Helps users think of alternative approaches
- **Legal Protection**: Reduces liability from incorrect advice

### Key Principle

This pattern bridges the gap between having boundaries and still being helpful by suggesting alternative ways to achieve the user's underlying goal within safe, clearly defined parameters.
