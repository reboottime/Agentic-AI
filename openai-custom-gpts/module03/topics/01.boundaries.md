# Boundaries

**Core Idea**: When creating a GPT, we must assume unexpected things will happen

- things may go wrong from our end
- users may not follow exactly expected interaction patterns and paths

Therefore, our GPT should **have clearly defined operational boundaries and robust mechanisms to handle edge cases when those boundaries are touched**.

Problem (dimensions) examples:

- exceeds its capability boundaries
- does things that are not in its capability list or goals
- user's intention is not clearly passed a clarity threshold
