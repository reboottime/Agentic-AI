# Vocabulary

- excerpts: 摘录
- Converse:
- Amicability: 友善
- red tape: "red tape" refers to excessive and unnecessary rules, regulations, and official procedures that cause delays and make getting things done complicated and time-consuming.
- valve: escape valve
- dimensions vs factors
  - Factor suggests a single variable in a calculation — narrow, often additive.
  - Dimension suggests a broader axis of influence, each with multiple underlying factors
- lecture:
  - Traditionally, a lecture means a professor speaking to a class in person
  - on `Coursera`, a short video is called a lecture
