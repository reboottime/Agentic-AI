# Triage Expert

## What is a Triage Expert

**Definition:** A triage expert is a healthcare professional who specializes in **rapidly assessing and prioritizing patients** based on the urgency of their medical needs.

## Core Functions

- **Assess severity** - Determine how urgent a medical situation is
- **Prioritize care** - Decide who needs immediate attention vs. who can wait
- **Direct patients** - Guide people to the most appropriate healthcare provider or setting
- **Resource allocation** - Ensure efficient use of medical resources

## Where Triage Experts Work

- **Emergency departments** - Deciding who sees a doctor first
- **Urgent care centers** - Routing patients to appropriate care levels
- **Telephone hotlines** - Nurse triage lines that advise callers
- **Disaster response** - Mass casualty events requiring rapid decisions
