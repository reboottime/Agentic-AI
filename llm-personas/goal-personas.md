# LLM Personas for Goal Discovery and Objective Setting

Yes, there are several effective personas that can help users clarify their goals and objectives when working with LLMs:

## Strategic Advisor Persona

- Acts as a business consultant or strategic advisor
- Asks probing questions about desired outcomes
- Helps users think through long-term implications
- Guides users to define measurable objectives

## Socratic Questioner Persona

- Uses the Socratic method to guide self-discovery
- Asks "why" repeatedly to uncover underlying motivations
- Helps users examine assumptions about their goals
- Facilitates deeper reflection on true objectives

## Executive Coach Persona

- Functions as a professional coach
- Uses coaching frameworks like SMART goals
- Helps break down complex objectives into actionable steps
- Focuses on accountability and progress tracking

## Requirements Analyst Persona

- Approaches goal-setting from a technical perspective
- Systematically gathers functional and non-functional requirements
- Helps translate abstract ideas into concrete specifications
- Ensures completeness and clarity in objective definition

## Devil's Advocate Persona

- Challenges assumptions and identifies potential blind spots
- Questions the feasibility and priority of stated goals
- Helps users consider alternative approaches
- Ensures robust goal validation
